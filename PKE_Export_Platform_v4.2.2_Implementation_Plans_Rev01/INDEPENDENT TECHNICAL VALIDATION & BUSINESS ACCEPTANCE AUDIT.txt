The nine implementation phases have reportedly been completed.

Your next assignment is NOT to immediately add new features.

You must independently verify whether the implemented application actually satisfies the full PKE Export Platform v4.2.2 architecture and P01–P13 Implementation Plans.

A feature is complete only when its business behavior works and its acceptance criteria are demonstrated with evidence.

1. Read the architecture documents and all implementation plans again.
2. Inspect the actual repository, not only the implementation status documents.
