# China — Feed ingredient import and overseas manufacturer requirements

## Chunk ID: CN-FEED-001 — Separate food and feed regimes
GACC Decree 280, effective 1 June 2026, concerns overseas manufacturers producing, processing or storing food for export to China. It replaces Decree 248. Do not apply it automatically to PKE merely because PKE is an agricultural product. PKE's intended feed use must be assessed under the applicable feed rules and any relevant bilateral inspection/quarantine protocol.
Source: GACC Decree 280, official text reproduced by MOFCOM.

## Chunk ID: CN-FEED-002 — Imported feed registration candidate
The official MOFCOM reproduction of Ministry of Agriculture Order 2014 No. 2 states that a foreign enterprise exporting feed/feed additives to China for the first time must obtain an import registration certificate before the product is sold or used, with application handled through an in-China agent. The text describes origin approval, product composition, manufacturing process, quality standards, testing and labeling evidence. Because this is a 2014 instrument with subsequent amendments and currentness not yet reconciled, it is a candidate rule requiring current MARA legal verification, not an active automated blocker.
Source: MOFCOM 2014 imported feed registration measures.

## Chunk ID: CN-FEED-003 — Technical dossier evidence
MARA's 2013 guidance lists product composition and properties, manufacturing flow, quality standards, three-batch samples and test reports, intended use, label/packaging, storage and shelf-life information. It is dated 2013 and explicitly says the Chinese original is authoritative; retain it as historical dossier guidance only until reconciled with current instruments.
Source: MARA Data Requirements for Registration of Imported Feed.

## Chunk ID: CN-FEED-004 — Fail-closed decision
For a PKE shipment to China, collect exact product category, intended use, producer/facility, country approval, Chinese importer/agent, product registration evidence and applicable bilateral protocol. If the applicable legal route is not verified, return PENDING_AUTHORITY_CONFIRMATION; do not infer a Decree 280 food registration obligation or claim feed registration is unnecessary.
