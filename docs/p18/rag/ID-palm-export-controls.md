# Indonesia — Palm commodity export controls and customs process

## Chunk ID: ID-PALM-001 — 2026 regime applicability
Permendag 16/2026 establishes Indonesia's strategic natural-resource palm commodity export regime and has been in force since 1 June 2026. KMK 33/MK/BC/2026 provides the corresponding customs list of restricted goods. This corpus does not yet contain a complete authoritative product/HS annex transcription. Do not conclude that PKE is included or excluded until its precise BTKI code and the operative annex row have been matched.
Sources: JDIH Kemendag Permendag 16/2026; JDIH Kemenkeu KMK 33/MK/BC/2026.

## Chunk ID: ID-PALM-002 — Customs export workflow
DJBC general export guidance describes goods preparation and supporting documents, PEB submission, risk-based physical examination when selected, customs document review, movement/loading and carrier departure. The page cites PMK 155/PMK.04/2022 as the export customs procedure basis. This general process does not replace commodity-specific restrictions or destination-country conditions.
Source: DJBC Tata Laksana Ekspor.

## Chunk ID: ID-PALM-003 — Classification and decision safeguards
A compliance assessment must preserve exact product identity, processing description, intended use, declared BTKI/HS code, origin, destination, shipment date and source version. If product classification or annex matching is unresolved, return PENDING_CLASSIFICATION or LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED, never a binary compliant/blocked result.
