# International — Good animal feeding and feed ingredient safety

## Chunk ID: INT-FEED-001 — Supply-chain scope
Codex CXC 54-2004 is the Code of Practice on Good Animal Feeding. The Codex catalogue shows a 2024 update. Its scope covers feed safety through procurement, handling, storage, processing and distribution of feed and feed ingredients for food-producing animals.
Source: Codex CXC 54-2004 official catalogue and PDF.

## Chunk ID: INT-FEED-002 — Operational use
Use the code as a reference framework for supplier approval, material acceptance, segregation, handling, storage, process controls, traceability and distribution evidence. Convert each control into a PKE-specific checklist only after technical/quality owners confirm applicability.

## Chunk ID: INT-FEED-003 — Legal status boundary
Codex is an international standard-setting reference, not automatically a binding national import/export rule. Do not label a shipment non-compliant solely because a Codex recommendation is not represented in a local law or contract.
