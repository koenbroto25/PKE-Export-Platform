# GACC Decree 280 — Registration of Overseas Manufacturers for Export of Food to China

Official sources:
- GACC text reproduced by PRC Ministry of Commerce: https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2026/art_769cdf8924364e359c387ba0fa1564d5.html
- English text published by PRC Consulate-General: https://adelaide.china-consulate.gov.cn/eng/jmhz_5_1/202604/t20260409_11888855.htm

Verified provisions:
- Promulgated 14 October 2025; effective 1 June 2026; replaces GACC Decree 248.
- Regulates registration of overseas manufacturers producing, processing or storing food for export to China.
- GACC classifies manufacturers and determines registration methods and evidence based on risk.
- Manufacturers exporting products in the official list must obtain recommendation from the competent authority of the country of origin.
- GACC may assess through document review, video inspection or on-site inspection.
- Registration is generally valid for five years and registration details are published by GACC.

RAG applicability guard:
Do not automatically apply this food-manufacturer regime to PKE as an animal-feed ingredient. Determine whether the specific product and intended use fall under food rules or the separate feed regulatory regime. Retrieval locators: Articles 2–7, 12–16 and 33.
