# China — Data Requirements for Registration of Imported Feed and Feed Additives

Official source: https://english.moa.gov.cn/policies/201910/t20191009_297869.html

Captured guidance:
The Ministry page lists an application form, proof that the product is approved in the country of origin, authorization for the in-China agent, composition and physicochemical description, manufacturing process, quality standards, inspection procedures, samples from three batches and test reports, intended use and usage precautions, label/packaging/storage/shelf-life information. It describes pre-review, quality re-check, possible feeding and safety tests, expert assessment and approval.

The page says the English version is provided for reference and the Chinese original is authoritative.
