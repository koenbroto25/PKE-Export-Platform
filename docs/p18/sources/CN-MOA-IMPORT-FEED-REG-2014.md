# China — Measures for Registration and Administration of Imported Feed and Feed Additives (2014 Order No. 2)

Official source: https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2014/art_e7a36e5fd69a4d64896d3fa5635c24ed.html

Captured provisions:
The text defines feed to include industrially processed products intended for animal consumption, including single feed ingredients, compound feed and concentrates. It states that a foreign enterprise exporting feed/feed additives to China for the first time must apply for import registration and obtain an import registration certificate before the product may be sold or used in China. Applications are handled through an in-China representative or agent.

The described dossier includes proof of approval in the country of origin, product composition and physicochemical properties, manufacturing process, quality standards, inspection methods and reports, labels, intended use and usage instructions. The text also describes sample testing, registration validity and renewal, and Chinese-language labeling.
