# Tata Laksana Ekspor — DJBC

Official source: https://onewebfile.beacukai.go.id/preview/tata-laksana-ekspor

Captured procedure summary:
DJBC export guidance identifies preparation of goods and supporting documents, submission of Pemberitahuan Ekspor Barang (PEB), risk-managed physical examination when selected, customs document review, movement into the customs area and loading, and carrier departure. The page identifies PMK 155/PMK.04/2022 as the export customs procedure regulation. It explains that goods loaded on a carrier for departure from the customs territory are legally treated as exported under the customs-law framework.

RAG limitation:
This is general customs procedure guidance. It does not establish PKE-specific licensing, export duty, product restrictions, phytosanitary conditions or destination-country requirements.
