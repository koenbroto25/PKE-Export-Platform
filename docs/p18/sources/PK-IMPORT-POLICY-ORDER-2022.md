# Pakistan — Import Policy Order 2022

Official sources:
- Trade Information Portal of Pakistan: https://www.tipp.gov.pk/ur-pk/site/display/80
- Ministry of Commerce SRO index: https://www.commerce.gov.pk/sros/
- SRO 704(I)/2026 official PDF: https://www.commerce.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/SRO-704.pdf

Captured policy logic:
TIPP states imports may be made from worldwide sources unless goods are prohibited or restricted by the Order. It records restrictions and prohibitions, including specified goods and animal-origin feed ingredients in defined BSE-risk circumstances. The Ministry SRO index shows repeated amendments, including 2025 and 2026 instruments. SRO 704(I)/2026 adds a forced-labour-related import prohibition provision.

PKE-specific caution:
Do not infer a PKE import prohibition from the animal-origin feed restriction. PKE is plant-derived, but exact classification, country of origin, sanitary/quarantine conditions and current amendments still require validation by the Pakistani importer and competent authorities. Use the consolidated Order and amendments effective on the shipment date.
